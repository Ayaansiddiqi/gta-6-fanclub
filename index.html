Gta VI

GTA 6 takes players to the vibrant streets of Vice City in the biggest and most ambitious Grand Theft Auto experience yet. Follow Jason and Lucia through a story filled with crime, chaos, adventure, and the unpredictable world of Leonida.

A skilled criminal trying to build a better life, Jason finds himself pulled deeper into the dangerous world of Leonida.

Lucia Caminos

Strong-willed and ambitious, Lucia is determined to change her life while navigating the risks of the criminal underworld.

Brian Heder

A seasoned criminal with connections across Leonida, Brian plays an important role in Jason's life and criminal ventures.

Cal Hampton

Jason's friend and a paranoid conspiracy enthusiast, Cal brings a chaotic and unpredictable energy to the story.
